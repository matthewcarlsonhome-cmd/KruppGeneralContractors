# Krupp Writing Standards

## Tone
- Professional and confident without arrogance
- Clear and direct — say what you mean in as few words as possible
- Client communications: warm but formal
- Internal documents: direct, factual, action-oriented

## Terminology
- Use "owner" or "client" consistently within a single document
- Refer to our company as "Krupp" or "Krupp General Contractors" (not "we" or "us" in formal documents)
- Use standard construction terminology — do not over-simplify for non-construction audiences
- Abbreviations: Define on first use, then abbreviate (e.g., "Request for Information (RFI)")

## Document Rules
- Use active voice: "Krupp completed the concrete pour" not "The concrete pour was completed"
- Numbers: Spell out one through nine, use numerals for 10 and above. Always use numerals for measurements, money, and percentages.
- Dates: Use "February 13, 2026" in client-facing documents, "2026-02-13" in internal tracking
- Money: Always include cents for values under $1,000. Use commas for thousands.
- Be specific with quantities: "85 cubic yards of concrete" not "a large amount of concrete"

## What to Avoid
- Jargon that obscures meaning
- Passive constructions that hide responsibility
- Vague time references ("soon", "shortly") — use specific dates
- Exclamation points in formal documents
- Emojis in any professional document
