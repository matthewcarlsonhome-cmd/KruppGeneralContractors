# Krupp General Contractors

## Company Overview
Krupp General Contractors is a full-service general contractor and construction manager. We deliver commercial, healthcare, education, and municipal projects with a commitment to quality, safety, and schedule reliability.

## Capabilities
- Pre-construction services including estimating, value engineering, and constructability review
- General contracting (hard bid, negotiated, design-build)
- Construction management (at-risk and agency)
- Self-performed concrete, carpentry, and general trades work

## Differentiators
- Experienced project management team with deep owner-side relationships
- Strong safety culture with consistently low EMR
- Technology-forward approach to project documentation and reporting
- Track record of on-time, on-budget delivery

## Core Values
- Safety is non-negotiable
- Deliver what we promise
- Build relationships, not just buildings
- Continuous improvement through lessons learned
