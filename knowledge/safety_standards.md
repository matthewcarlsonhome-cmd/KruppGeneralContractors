# Krupp Safety Standards

## Safety Policy
Safety is Krupp's highest priority. Every worker goes home safe every day — no exceptions. We comply with all applicable OSHA standards (29 CFR 1926 for construction) and maintain a site-specific safety plan for every project.

## Key Requirements
- All workers must complete site-specific safety orientation before beginning work
- Daily toolbox safety talks are required on every active project
- PPE requirements: hard hat, safety glasses, high-visibility vest, and steel-toed boots at minimum
- Fall protection required at 6 feet or greater (OSHA 1926.501)
- 100% tie-off policy for all elevated work
- Confined space entry requires permit and trained personnel

## Incident Reporting
- All incidents, including near misses, must be reported immediately to the superintendent
- First aid cases documented same-day
- OSHA recordable incidents reported within 24 hours to the safety director
- Fatalities reported to OSHA within 8 hours (1-800-321-OSHA)
- Amputations, loss of eye, or in-patient hospitalization reported within 24 hours

## OSHA Reference Standards
- 29 CFR 1926 Subpart C — General Safety and Health Provisions
- 29 CFR 1926 Subpart E — Personal Protective Equipment
- 29 CFR 1926 Subpart K — Electrical
- 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M — Fall Protection
- 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P — Excavations
- 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC — Cranes and Derricks
